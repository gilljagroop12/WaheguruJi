The Name

Two words, spoken as one

Not a description and not a title. A name said in wonder — and the small word of respect that never leaves its side.

ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ

Waheguru

Wonder itself, spoken as a name. Wahe is the gasp before words arrive; Guru is the light that moves a person from darkness into understanding. To say it is already to praise.

ਜੀ

Ji

Love and respect, carried in a single syllable. It is never demanded and never left out — the difference between speaking about someone and speaking to them.

Mool Mantar

The root of it all

The opening verse of the Guru Granth Sahib, revealed by Guru Nanak Dev Ji. Before ritual, before rule — a description of the One.

ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ
ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ
ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥

Ik Onkar · Sat Naam · Karta Purakh
Nirbhau · Nirvair · Akaal Moorat
Ajooni · Saibhang · Gur Prasaad

One creator, whose name is truth.
Doer of all things, without fear, without hatred.
Beyond time, beyond birth and death, self-existent —
known by the grace of the Guru.

Three Pillars

How a life is built

Guru Nanak Dev Ji left three instructions, plain enough for anyone and demanding enough for a lifetime.

ਨਾਮ ਜਪੋ

Naam Japo

Keep the Name on the breath. Remembrance is not an hour set aside — it is a thread carried through the ordinary day, under the noise, steady as a pulse.

ਕਿਰਤ ਕਰੋ

Kirat Karo

Earn honestly, by your own hands and effort. Work is not separate from worship. A life supported by fair labour is itself an offering.

ਵੰਡ ਛਕੋ

Vand Chhako

Share before you take. What comes to you was never only yours — the measure of a household is what leaves it for someone who has less.

Living It

Words that become habits

ਸੇਵਾ

Sewa

Service given without wanting to be seen giving it. The sweeping of a floor counts as much as the building of a hall.

ਸਿਮਰਨ

Simran

Loving remembrance. Not recitation for its own sake, but attention turned gently and repeatedly toward the One.

ਸੰਗਤ

Sangat

The company you keep shapes what you become. Sit among those who are seeking, and the seeking becomes easier.

ਲੰਗਰ

Langar

The free kitchen, open to every person. All seated on the same floor, at the same level, eating the same food — equality made edible.

ਚੜ੍ਹਦੀ ਕਲਾ

Chardi Kala

Spirits that rise. Not forced cheer, but a stubborn, unbroken optimism that holds even when the news is bad.

ਸਰਬੱਤ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ

Sarbat da Bhala

The wellbeing of all. Every prayer ends by asking not for oneself, but for everyone — including those who would not ask for you.
